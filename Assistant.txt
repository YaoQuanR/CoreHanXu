Covered language translation and platform migration scenarios.

Translation work:
Translation is considered as "adaptation". Any unauthorized republishing of a translation will be treated as copyright infringement.
If you seek authorization to translation:
1. Republish:
    - You are required to send the author a formal request email, which includes a brief introduction about yourself and your intended scope.
    - You should wait for the author's reply of acceptance before proceeding.
    - You should clearly display proof of your authorization includes time & name on the publication page.
    - When you republish work is done, email the author a list of links.
    - Usually, you must license your work under AGPL and includes your name as required by license.txt.
    - Also, your translation work is considered a derivative version.
    - Unless authorization revoke due to valid complaints, you remain responsible for the translation.

2. Co-work:
    - You are required to send the author a formal request email, which includes a brief introduction about yourself,
      a list of responsible members, and your intended scope.
    - You may submit your translated language file in the request email, or other possible download link.
    - You will receive a reply of acceptance, and may receive further update message for your translation work.
    - In further publish, your member list will be announced to be responsible member for handling request.
    - Unless authorization revoke due to valid complaints, you remain responsible for the translation.

Migration work:
Under the license of CC BY-NC-ND, any work that includes textures/sounds without clearly authorization is considered infringement.
If you seek authorization to migration:
1. Platform migration to other Minecraft Java Edition version:
    - Your work is considered a derivative version.
      Thus, you are required to license your program under the license of AGPL.
    - You should not modify the original resources, or otherwise constitutes infringement.
    - Notifying the author is optional, but you must include your name as required by license.txt.

2. Platform migration to Minecraft Bedrock Edition version or/and
   Minecraft NetEase (Chinese) Edition version included modified Java/Bedrock Edition:
    - Your work is considered a derivative version.
    - You are required to send the author a formal request email, which includes a brief introduction about yourself and your intended scope.
    - You should wait for the author's reply of acceptance before proceeding.
    - You should clearly display proof of your authorization includes time & name on the publication page.
    - When you republish work is done, email the author a list of links.
    - Usually, you must license your work under AGPL and includes your name as required by license.txt.
    - This procedure is to ensure you authorized to use CC BY-NC-ND resources to other platform.
      Otherwise, your work will be treated as copyright infringement.
    - *For NetEase (Chinese) Edition version only:
        All additional package that depend on this version must also be licensed under an OSI-approved license.

Further explanation:
- Derivative work: Derivative and adaptation is an end use type that requires authorization.
- Assistant: Republish (any platform)/ Modification (any%)/ Reuse CC BY-NC-ND resources (any%)/ Translation (any%).
